Why creators need a shot list

Even a 10-row list saves reshoots. You know which inserts, reactions, and detail shots to grab before you wrap. Editors (including you, later) get coverage instead of hoping stock will fix gaps.

Free template columns

Copy these columns into Sheets, Notion, or a notes app:

#ShotFramingMoveAudio~TimeNotes
1Hook openMCUStaticVO / mic5sLook to camera
2Product heroCUSlow pushRoom tone4sClean background
3Hands demoOTSHandheldNat sound8sShow button press
  • Framing: ECU / CU / MCU / MS / WS
  • Move: static, pan, tilt, push, pull, handheld, gimbal
  • Audio: VO, lav, boom, nat sound, music bed later
  • ~Time: rough intended screen time, not camera roll time

Examples by video type

  • Tutorial - hook, problem, steps as inserts, result, CTA
  • Product review - unbox, hero, detail macros, in-use, alternatives
  • Talking head - A-roll angles, cutaways for pacing, end screen plate
  • Short-form hook - cold open visual, punch line, loopable end frame
