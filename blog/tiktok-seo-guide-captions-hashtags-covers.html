TikTok is a search engine with a For You feed attached. Treat captions, spoken hooks, hashtags, and covers like packaging - not an afterthought.

What TikTok SEO means in 2026

People search TikTok for how-tos, products, and entertainment. Signals that help matching include:

  • Caption text and keywords used naturally
  • On-screen text and spoken words in the audio
  • Watch time, replays, and shares
  • Cover image clarity in profile grids and search

You cannot “hack” the algorithm with 30 unrelated hashtags. You can make the topic obvious.

Caption copy that helps discovery

  • Lead with the topic or outcome in the first line.
  • Add context for skimmers (who it is for, what they get).
  • Keep it scannable - line breaks beat a wall of text.
  • Include a soft CTA that matches the content (“Save for your next shoot”).

Hashtag strategy that is not spam

  1. 1 broad topic tag (e.g. #VideoEditing)
  2. 2–4 specific niche tags that match the video
  3. Skip irrelevant trending tags that confuse the topic

Covers and first frames

One-brief packaging workflow

  1. Write a brief: topic, audience, promise, platforms.
  2. Draft TikTok caption + hashtags (and YouTube/IG variants if you cross-post).
  3. Pick a cover frame or generate cover concepts.
  4. Publish with on-screen captions if the video needs them.

Frequently asked questions

Does TikTok have SEO?

Yes - captions, on-screen text, speech, and engagement help match videos to search and feeds.

How many hashtags should I use?

A small relevant set beats a long spam list.

Captions vs burned-in subtitles?

Caption field = discovery text under the post. Burned-in subtitles = on-screen text for silent viewing.
