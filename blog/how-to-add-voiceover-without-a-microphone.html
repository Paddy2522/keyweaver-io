How to Add Voiceover to a Video Without a Microphone

No mic, no booth, still need narration. Here is a clean way to add AI voiceover to a video in 2026 without pretending it is a human recordist.

On this page

  1. Your options without a mic
  2. Write a speakable script
  3. Generate inside AE or Premiere
  4. Mix so it feels intentional
  5. Disclosure and ethics
  6. FAQ

Your options without a microphone

  1. AI voiceover in your NLE - fastest for drafts and many social videos (Trillian).
  2. Phone voice memos - better than silence; still need cleanup.
  3. Hire remote talent - best for hero brand spots.

This guide focuses on option 1 when you need something shippable today.

Write a speakable script

  • Short sentences. One idea per line.
  • Avoid long URL strings and dense numbers.
  • Read it out loud once - if you stumble, the model will too.

Generate inside After Effects or Premiere

  1. Install Trillian for your host from Keyweaver Manager.
  2. Paste the script, pick a voice, check the credit estimate.
  3. Generate and import audio onto the timeline.
  4. Add captions with Cuemark (AE) if the video is watched muted.

Mix so AI VO feels intentional

  • Leave breathing room - do not wall-to-wall talk over music.
  • Duck beds with Tambourine or your usual track.
  • Keep levels consistent across cuts.

Disclosure and ethics

Follow platform rules and client policies for synthetic media. For many educational and draft uses, AI VO is normal - for testimonials and news, prefer real humans.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add voiceover without a mic?

Use an AI voiceover tool inside After Effects or Premiere Pro, such as Trillian: paste a script, generate narration, and import audio to the timeline.

Is AI voiceover good enough for YouTube?

For many tutorials and explainers, yes - especially with a tight script and clean mix. Hero brand spots may still need hired talent.

Can I caption AI voiceover automatically?

In After Effects, generate VO with Trillian then caption with Cuemark. That keeps narration and subtitles in one AE workflow.
