After Effects vs Premiere Pro AI Plugins

Choosing AI plugins is easier when you decide by host. Here is how After Effects and Premiere Pro differ - and which Keyweaver tools install on each.

Different jobs, different hosts

After Effects wins for designed social packages, kinetic captions, and motion finishing. Premiere Pro wins for long timelines, multicam, and dialogue cutting. AI plugins should respect that split instead of forcing one app.

Why shared credits matter

If VO lives in Premiere and captions finish in AE, you do not want two unrelated wallets. Keyweaver Credits are shared across Cuemark, Trillian, Superconductor, and Tambourine. See pricing.

What to install first

  • Mostly AE social → Cuemark + Ludo, then Trillian
  • Mostly Premiere YouTube → Trillian + Tambourine, then Superconductor
  • Hybrid shops → Manager both tabs; same account everywhere

Frequently asked questions

Do After Effects AI plugins work in Premiere Pro?

Not always. Cuemark and Ludo are After Effects only. Trillian, Tambourine, and Superconductor offer both After Effects and Premiere Pro builds.

Should I buy AI tools for AE or Premiere first?

Install for the host where you spend the most time. Hybrid teams should prefer suites with shared credits across both apps.

How do I install Premiere AI plugins from Keyweaver?

Open Keyweaver Manager, select the Premiere Pro tab, install Trillian, Tambourine, or Superconductor, then fully quit and reopen Premiere.

Install for AE, Premiere, or both

Keyweaver Manager has After Effects and Premiere Pro tabs. One account, shared credits for the AI panels.
