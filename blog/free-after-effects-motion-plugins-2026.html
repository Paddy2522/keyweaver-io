Free forever vs free trial

Many "free After Effects plugins" are trials. For motion design utilities you want no credit card and no cloud meter. AI transcription and generative video usually cannot be free forever because they have real API cost - but local animation tools can.

Ludo: free Keyweaver motion toolkit

Ludo is free and does not use Keyweaver Credits. Typical uses:

  • Animation ease curves and strength controls
  • Text split (characters/words), stagger, typewriter-style animate-on
  • Keyframe stagger / align / reverse / distribute
  • Cloner-style layout helpers
  • Colour palette tools
  • Frame export and GIF export from the work area

Install from the After Effects tab in Keyweaver Manager.

Other free helpers editors still use

  • Native AE align, graph editor, and text animators
  • Community expressions (prefer tools that do not inject vendor-branded expression spam)
  • Free preset packs - quality varies; always check license

When paid AI still makes sense

Use free motion tools for craft. Add paid AI when the bottleneck is content: captions (Cuemark), VO (Trillian), B-roll (Superconductor), or music (Tambourine). Guest trials let you test before buying credits.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free After Effects plugin in 2026?

For everyday motion work, Ludo is a strong free toolkit covering animation, text, keyframes, layout, colour, and export - with no credits required.

Are Keyweaver plugins free?

Ludo is free. Cuemark, Trillian, Superconductor, and Tambourine install free and offer guest trials, then use shared Keyweaver Credits for AI generation.

Do free AE plugins work with Premiere Pro?

Ludo is After Effects only. Keyweaver AI audio/video companions (Trillian, Tambourine, Superconductor) also offer Premiere builds.
