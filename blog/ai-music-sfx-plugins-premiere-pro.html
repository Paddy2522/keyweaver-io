The mid-edit audio problem

Picture is locked enough to need sound, but opening a full music library breaks flow. An AI music plugin for Premiere Pro should prompt a bed or SFX, preview cost, and import onto the sequence.

What to look for

  • Separate controls for music beds vs one-shot SFX
  • Duration and mood in the prompt
  • Credit estimate before generate
  • AE companion if you finish motion in After Effects

Tambourine for Premiere Pro and After Effects

Tambourine is Keyweaver's AI music and SFX panel. Install the Premiere build from Manager when you cut in Premiere; use the AE build when you design in After Effects. Credits are shared with Cuemark, Trillian, and Superconductor.

Fast music / SFX workflow

  1. Mark the section that needs underscoring or a hit.
  2. Open Tambourine → describe mood, length, and type (bed vs SFX).
  3. Generate → import → duck under dialogue.
  4. Iterate with a tighter prompt if the first bed is too busy.

Licensing note
