FAQ

Common questions

How are credits charged?

Trillian shows an estimate before you generate. Cost scales with script length. Credits come from your shared Keyweaver balance.

Do I need an account?

You can try a guest trial in the panel. Sign up free for 40 shared credits that follow you across AI plugins.

Where does the audio go?

Generated narration imports into your After Effects composition or Premiere Pro sequence so you can time it with picture.

Is there a Premiere build?

Yes. Install Trillian from the After Effects tab or Trillian for Premiere from the Premiere Pro tab in Keyweaver Manager.
